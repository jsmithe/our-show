# Streaming availability in Our Show

Research for [issue #10](https://github.com/jsmithe/our-show/issues/10) · November 24, 2026 · United States

**Recommendation.** Use TMDB for a U.S. subscription hint in Tonight and on the confirmed pick, pending an authenticated API smoke test. Show “Listed on Disney+” when a current lookup lists a direct subscription that matches a household selection. Keep provider information out of ranking and filtering. A title listing helps a parent find the show, while the service still determines what their account can play.

## What Our Show does today

The deployed TV search returned the expected TMDB IDs for all five sample titles without provider fields. On [main at `7132390`](https://github.com/jsmithe/our-show/commit/7132390feca38d28dd12d7863ea283fdf4e66472), the [server-side search](../../lib/tmdb.ts) calls only TMDB’s `/search/tv` with a server-held token. It stores title metadata and artwork under `tmdb:<id>`. The [search card](../../components/browse.tsx#L113) prints “Availability unknown” for every result. The Bluey and Puffin Rock results described in the issue reflect this code path, so selecting Netflix could never resolve them.

[Household settings](../../components/household-settings.tsx#L5) store four optional service names in browser-local household data and backups, with no country or entitlement check. [Tonight](../../components/tonight.tsx#L45) uses the same [metadata label](../../components/shared.tsx#L22) before and after confirmation. Starter Bluey and Puffin Rock use `mock-*` IDs and show sample labels. The [current ranker](../../lib/household.ts#L201) uses a matching sample service as the last tie-breaker after viewer preferences. Add deduplication can also keep a starter or manual entry in place when the same TMDB title is found later, so that saved entry can still lack a TMDB ID.

## What TMDB provides

- [TV Series Watch Providers](https://developer.themoviedb.org/reference/tv-series-watch-providers) returns provider offers by country for `GET /3/tv/{series_id}/watch/providers`. Read `results.US`. The [TV provider directory](https://developer.themoviedb.org/reference/watch-provider-tv-list) takes `watch_region=US` and supplies the provider IDs to validate. TMDB also documents [supported regions](https://developer.themoviedb.org/reference/watch-providers-available-regions) and an equivalent [season lookup](https://developer.themoviedb.org/reference/tv-season-watch-providers).
- `flatrate` is subscription streaming. Keep it distinct from `rent`, `buy`, `free`, and `ads`. A paid subscription tier with ads can still appear in `flatrate`. Amazon Video purchases and Amazon channel subscriptions have IDs distinct from base Prime Video. The TV [Discover API](https://developer.themoviedb.org/reference/discover-tv) supports region, provider, and monetization filters if discovery ever needs them.
- The regional API `link` leads to TMDB’s Watch page. Individual entries do not supply playback links. Label the link “Check where to watch”. Credit JustWatch near the provider information and retain the existing [TMDB logo and notice](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq).
- [JustWatch](https://media.justwatch.com/content-insights) says it refreshes its own data at least every 24 hours. TMDB’s endpoint documentation supplies no per-offer timestamp or contractual update interval. The app can record when it fetched a response. A successful fetch leaves upstream freshness, plan access, and episode coverage uncertain.

## U.S. evidence

Checked November 24, 2026 using TMDB’s public Watch pages with an explicit U.S. region and signed-out first-party listings. The separately checked UK Bluey page included BBC iPlayer, which the U.S. page did not list. All five live API probes returned HTTP 401 without a credential. The production app exposes title search only, and the research environment had no API credential. **Confidence in the recommendation.** High for the app diagnosis and the usefulness of a qualified positive hint. Medium for production readiness until the live API is sampled. Low for inferring account access or absence from a series-level result. Direct [JustWatch partner data](https://apis.justwatch.com/docs/content_partner/) could add offer detail and links where TMDB falls short, with the same upstream catalog and partner setup. [Watchmode](https://api.watchmode.com/use-cases/app-deep-links) offers provider and episode links under its [plan terms](https://api.watchmode.com/pricing). Revisit either if provider deep links or season precision become requirements. Use the sampled provider listings to judge coverage before paying for a replacement.

## Acceptance criteria for a follow-up

1. **Validate before rollout.** From an authorized server environment, capture a sanitized U.S. response and regional link for each of the five IDs, and confirm the mapping against the live U.S. provider directory. Compare with the then-current first-party listings. A disputed title/service combination remains unknown until reconciled. Repeat the UK Bluey control to catch a region fallback.
2. **Match direct subscription IDs.** Use an explicit allowlist of U.S. `flatrate` IDs and deduplicate tiers to their household service. Historical seed IDs for validation are Netflix `8` and `1796`, Prime Video `9` and `2100`, Disney+ `337`, and Paramount+ `531`, `2303`, and `2616`. For example, Amazon Video `10`, Paramount+ Amazon Channel `582`, and PBS KIDS Amazon Channel `293` never satisfy the base Prime Video selection. Cover buy-only, rent-only, free or ads only, add-ons, and genuine paid ad-supported tiers in verification.
3. **Keep the lookup narrow.** Fetch providers server-side for the visible suggestion or confirmed series, using the existing server-side credential. Key the cache by TMDB ID and country. Start with a six-hour cache lifetime and hide an expired positive until a refresh succeeds. Keep the household selections in the browser for matching. Missing or malformed U.S. results, timeouts, rate limits, and missing IDs lead to an unknown state. Use curated exact IDs for starter Bluey and Puffin Rock while preserving their saved shelf entries. Unlinked manual entries and legacy channels stay unknown.
4. **Show a compact hint.** With a current selected-service match, use one line such as “Listed on Netflix” in the Tonight card and the same wording after confirmation. Collapse duplicates and keep the heading and action buttons stable while data loads. When the user selected services and there is no usable match, show “Availability unknown”. With no selected services, omit the status line. On confirmation, show “Check where to watch” when the response supplies a validated HTTPS TMDB Watch link for the same title and region. Use JustWatch attribution and verify that “Let’s watch” and “Not tonight” remain visible on the small-phone layout.
5. **Keep availability out of decisions.** Retire the sample-service tie-breaker. Changing service selections or availability results must yield the same ordered candidate IDs for the same viewer preferences and passes. The whole pass, confirm, undo, and reopen flow still works when every provider lookup fails.

**Owner approval before implementation.** Approve U.S.-only coverage and the meaning of the four household checkboxes as direct subscriptions, with add-ons and transactional offers outside the first release. Approve the display-only launch, including removal of the sample-service tie-breaker.
